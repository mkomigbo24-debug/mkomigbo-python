SH ISSUE - HOW TO BRING OUT NASALIZATION WITHOUT H?

Problem: SH is existing phoneme /ʃ/ rightly so. But SH as S+H is palatalized S, not nasalized S. SH will not work for nasal! Need different marker.

Solution:

1. Keep SH = /ʃ/ as legitimate palatal phoneme (rightly so)
2. For nasalization, do NOT use H! Use: tilde ~, ṅ (n dot), ⁿ, or '
3. Example: S̃ỤỌ /s̃ʷɔ/ = nasal S + ụ + ọ (different from SHỤỌ /ʃʷɔ/)
4. ZH /ʒ/ also palatal - keep! Nasal Z = Z̃ỤỌ /z̃ʷɔ/
5. This removes H overload: H = aspiration only, ~ = nasal, ṅ = nasal

Thesis update: H currently does 3 jobs - confusion! Propose: H=aspiration, ~ = nasalization, SH/ZH stay as palatal but recognized as S/Z + palatal marker, not nasal!
